# Agenthood v3.35.1

**Date:** August 19, 2026

Here's what's new in this patch release.

## v3.35.1

### Bug Fixes

- **Milestones:** The milestones plugin no longer writes CHANGELOG.md or release notes. Those files are now left entirely to semantic-release, preventing duplicate or conflicting entries during automated releases.
