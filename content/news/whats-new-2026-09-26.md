# Agenthood v3.68.0: Convert & Trust

**Date:** September 26, 2026
**Author:** Agenthood Team

## v3.68.0 — Convert & Trust

We've shipped v3.68.0, the Convert & Trust release. It focuses on three areas: onboarding, fail-closed skill trust, and cost observability. We've also fixed two dependency issues.

### Features

The Convert & Trust release ([#913](https://github.com/fworks-tech/agenthood/pull/913)) includes:

- **Onboarding:** Improvements to the onboarding experience.
- **Fail-closed skill trust:** Skills are now fail-closed. They will not execute unless explicitly trusted, which tightens the default security posture.
- **Cost observability:** New visibility into the cost of agent runs.

These changes close a number of long-standing issues, including #574, #619, #620, #578, #152, #151, #514, #515, #604, #606, #624, #625, #651, and #465.

### Bug Fixes

- **Dependency resolution:** Pinned tree-sitter-go to 0.23.4 so `npm ci` resolves without ERESOLVE errors ([#911](https://github.com/fworks-tech/agenthood/issues/911), [#910](https://github.com/fworks-tech/agenthood/issues/910)).
- **Lockfile peers:** Restored optional peers that were dropped from the lockfile in [#916](https://github.com/fworks-tech/agenthood/issues/916) ([#924](https://github.com/fworks-tech/agenthood/issues/924), [#910](https://github.com/fworks-tech/agenthood/issues/910)).
